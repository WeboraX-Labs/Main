Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. Overview

WeboraX Labs ("we", "us", "our") builds websites for local businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or use our website, and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us when you reach out — name, phone number, email, business name.
  • Project information you share to help us build your website, such as business hours, menus, service lists, images and logos.
  • Messages you send us over WhatsApp, email or any contact form on this site.

We don't use tracking cookies or analytics scripts that build an advertising profile of visitors to this page.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To design, build and maintain the website you've asked us for.
  • To send you updates about your project's progress.

We do not sell your information to third parties.

4. WhatsApp and email

When you message us on WhatsApp or email, that conversation is stored by WhatsApp/your email provider and by us, so we can keep track of your project. Standard WhatsApp and email privacy terms from those platforms also apply to those messages.

5. Sharing your information

We only share client information with third parties where necessary to build your website — for example, a hosting provider or a booking/payment tool you've asked us to integrate — and only to the extent needed for that purpose.

6. Data retention

We keep project information for as long as needed to deliver and support your website, or as long as you remain a client. You can ask us to delete information we hold about you at any time.

7. Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at weborax.labs@gmail.com or WhatsApp.
